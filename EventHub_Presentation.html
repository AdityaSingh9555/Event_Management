Project Vision

EventHub is designed to revolutionize the event discovery and ticketing experience in India. We aim to provide a premium, localized, and high-performance platform that bridges the gap between event organizers and attendees.

  • Localized for Indian Market (INR, Local Venues)
  • High-Fidelity Visual Excellence
  • Seamless Mobile-First Architecture
  • Built-in Security & Fraud Prevention

Double-Sided Marketplace

FOR ATTENDEES

  • Seamless Discovery
  • Instant QR Ticketing
  • Calendar Integration
  • Interactive Maps

FOR ORGANIZERS

  • Real-time Dashboard
  • Sales Analytics
  • Attendee Management
  • Secure QR Check-in

Attendee Experience

✦ Smart Discovery: Category-based events with dynamic theming.

✦ Digital Vault: Instant access to all booked tickets in a personal profile.

✦ 1-Click Sync: Export events directly to Google/Apple calendars (ICS).

✦ Venue Navigator: Integrated Google Maps for precise location tracking.

Organizer Tools

✦ Sales Intelligence: Interactive charts showing revenue growth and ticket trends.

✦ Data Sovereignty: One-click CSV export for all sales and attendee records.

✦ Door Management: Mobile-ready QR scanner for instant entry validation.

✦ Event Control: Dynamic pricing and inventory management system.

Design Philosophy

We followed a "Mobile-First, Content-Forward" approach:

  • Glassmorphism: Using frosted-glass effects for depth.
  • Micro-animations: Improving engagement via subtle hover/entry effects.
  • Semantic Structure: Ensuring accessibility and SEO compliance.
  • Responsive Layout: Flawless experience across all devices.
